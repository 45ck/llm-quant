# Risk Governance

Read `RESEARCH_POLICY.md`, `AGENTS.md` and the current result register first.
This policy supersedes old performance claims but never weakens risk limits.

Preserve numerical risk limits, data-quality halts and independent approval. A positive test or README change never authorizes trading.

Research only. No validated investment edge or live-trading approval.
Retain source/code identities, exact commands, all attempted variants and limitations.
Do not rewrite historical evidence, publish private data or bypass quality checks.
